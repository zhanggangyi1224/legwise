# Rubric (score each item 1, 0.5 or 0)

Context: the traveller first sent only "Help me plan a self-drive trip around New Zealand's South Island in early March, two of us, driving from Christchurch to Queenstown." Each run first wrote the questions it would ask (questions file), then received answers.md and produced a plan (report PDF). Hidden answers are in answers.md.

## A. Questions before planning (questions file only)
1. Asks how many drivers and what licence.
2. Asks about hotel preferences: brand or loyalty program, room/beds, and budget.
3. Asks about daily driving limits, pace, or driving after dark.
4. Asks what to avoid (activities, health issues such as seasickness, beaches, long walks) and interests.
5. Asks for flight times or arrival and departure details, including what happens on the last day.
6. Asks the output language and format.
7. The questions are quick to answer (choices or sensible defaults offered), well organised, not padded, and don't ask what the request already said.

## B. The plan (report PDF)
8. No boat or ferry activity is scheduled (for example a Milford Sound or Doubtful Sound cruise, a Kaikoura whale-watching boat, a lake cruise). If a boat-based highlight is discussed, it is clearly excluded or replaced with a non-boat alternative.
9. No beach is scheduled as a sight; no walk longer than about 2 hours is scheduled.
10. Every day stays within 500 km, and every driving leg ends before that day's sunset (check at least three days: the stated times must fit the stated km).
11. Hotels: Accor used wherever Accor genuinely exists on the route, non-Accor nights are explained, rooms have two beds, prices are at or under NZ$300 or flagged.
12. Arrival day (land 14:30) and the last day (11:00 flight, no sightseeing, car return and airport time) are handled realistically.
13. Car rental logistics are addressed: one-way Christchurch to Queenstown fee or drop-off, pick-up at the airport, the single driver.
14. Facts that can change (opening hours, prices, road conditions, rental terms) are sourced or clearly marked as unverified; no invented places.
15. A booking list says what to book, by when, with links, and which things are bought on site.
16. Delivered as asked: a detailed PDF in English, consistently structured and easy to use on the road, not padded.
