# A few questions before I plan your South Island drive

Early March is a good time to go. The summer crowds have thinned, the weather is usually settled, and it stays light until about 8 pm. Christchurch to Queenstown can be anything from a 6-hour drive to a two-week loop, so your answers here will set the shape of the plan. Short answers are fine. Pick a letter where there are options, and skip anything you don't mind about.

## 1. Dates and flights
1. What are your exact dates? Give the day you land in Christchurch and the day you fly out of Queenstown, or just the number of nights if you don't have dates yet.
2. Have you booked flights? If so, what time do you **arrive in Christchurch** and **leave Queenstown**? This tells me whether day 1 and the last day can include any driving.
3. Is the trip one-way (Christchurch in, Queenstown out), or could you return to Christchurch? A one-way rental often costs an extra relocation fee, but it's usually still the better choice.

## 2. Route
4. Which way would you like to go? There are two main routes.
   - **A. Inland/Alpine:** Lake Tekapo, then Aoraki/Mount Cook, then Wānaka. Shorter, with turquoise lakes, big mountains and dark-sky stargazing.
   - **B. West Coast:** Arthur's Pass, then the West Coast (Punakaiki, Hokitika), the Franz Josef and Fox glaciers, Haast Pass and Wānaka. Longer, with rainforest, beaches and glaciers, and more rain.
   - **C. Both, as a loop.** This needs about 10 nights or more.
   - **D. Not sure. Suggest one based on my days.**
5. Are any of these musts or optional extras? **Milford Sound** (a long day from Queenstown, or stay overnight in Te Anau), **Kaikōura** (whales, a detour north), **Akaroa** (dolphins, near Christchurch), **Dunedin/Catlins** (wildlife, southeast coast).
6. Is there anywhere you already know you want to go, or have been before and want to skip?

## 3. Drivers and vehicle
7. Will both of you drive, or only one? It matters for how long the driving days can be.
8. Where are your driving licences from? Are they in English, or will you need an International Driving Permit or an approved translation? Have you driven on the **left** before?
9. Car or campervan?
   - **A. Car, staying in hotels or motels**
   - **B. Campervan, staying at holiday parks**
   - **C. Undecided, help me compare**
10. Any preference for car size or type, such as an automatic, an SUV, or an EV/hybrid?

## 4. Pace and driving
11. What's the most driving you'd be happy with in one day?
    - **A.** About 2 to 3 hours. Relaxed, with lots of time at stops.
    - **B.** About 4 hours.
    - **C.** 5 hours or more is fine on some days.
12. Would you rather stay 2 or more nights in fewer places, or move on most days to see more?
13. Is driving after dark OK? I'd normally keep you off mountain passes at night.

## 5. Where you stay
14. What kind of place? **A.** Budget or motel **B.** Mid-range hotel **C.** Something special, such as a lodge or a room with a view **D.** A mix
15. Roughly what's your budget per night for the room, or for the whole trip? Which currency?
16. Do you have any hotel loyalty programmes or points you'd like to use? For example Accor, IHG, Hilton or Marriott.
17. What room do you need? For example one bed or two, or a kitchen or laundry partway through.

## 6. What you enjoy
18. Which of these interest you? Pick any.
    - Walks and hikes. Easy, like Hooker Valley Track, or harder, like Roy's Peak or the Ben Lomond track.
    - Glaciers, such as a heli-hike or a scenic flight
    - Stargazing (Tekapo dark-sky tours)
    - Wildlife, such as penguins, seals, whales or dolphins
    - Boat cruises (Milford or Doubtful Sound)
    - Adventure, such as bungy, jet boats, skydiving or canyon swings
    - Wineries and food (Central Otago, Gibbston)
    - Hot pools
    - Photography spots and lookouts
    - Small towns and history
19. What's your fitness like? How long is the longest walk you'd enjoy in a day?
20. Is there one experience that would make this trip a success for you?

## 7. Anything to avoid
21. Is there anything you'd rather avoid? For example gravel roads, steep or winding mountain roads, heights, boats or seasickness, very early starts, or crowds.
22. Do either of you have dietary needs, mobility issues or health conditions I should plan around?

## 8. Practical details
23. What are your **passport nationalities**? Some visitors need an **NZeTA** or a visa before they fly.
24. Have you booked anything already, such as the car, hotels or tours?
25. Do you have travel insurance, or would you like reminders about it and other things to sort before you go?

Once I have your answers, I'll build a day-by-day plan. It will have realistic drive times, a stop each night, the things worth booking early, and backup options for bad-weather days. Milford and the West Coast often get them.
