# Before I plan your Christchurch to Queenstown drive

Happy to plan this. Christchurch to Queenstown is a one-way drive, and how I order the stops depends on a few things. Please answer what you can. "No preference" is fine for any of them.

1. **Dates and number of days.** Which day do you pick up the car in Christchurch, and which day do you need to be finished in Queenstown? Or tell me the total number of days on the road.

2. **Must-visit stops.** Which places do you definitely want to include? Common choices on this route are:
   - Lake Tekapo / Lake Pukaki
   - Aoraki/Mount Cook (Hooker Valley Track)
   - Wanaka
   - The West Coast: Arthur's Pass, Punakaiki, Franz Josef / Fox Glacier, Haast Pass
   - Kaikōura (whales, seals)
   - Akaroa / Banks Peninsula
   - Dunedin and the Otago Peninsula
   - Milford Sound or Doubtful Sound (a day trip from Queenstown or Te Anau)
   - Anything else you have in mind

   For each one, is it a must-visit or nice-to-have?

3. **Route style.** Do you want the fastest direct inland route (Christchurch, Tekapo, Mount Cook, Wanaka, Queenstown), the longer scenic loop up and down the West Coast, or the east coast via Dunedin?

4. **Daily driving limit.** What is the most driving you're happy with in a day, in hours? Are you both driving, and is either of you new to driving on the left or on mountain roads?

5. **Fixed times and bookings.** Do you already have anything booked with a set time, such as flights in or out (arrival time in Christchurch, departure time from Queenstown), hotels, a Milford Sound cruise, a scenic flight or a glacier heli-hike?

6. **Time at each stop and pace.** Do you prefer quick photo stops, short walks (under 2 hours) or longer day hikes? Any stops where you already know you want a half day or more?

7. **Things to find along the way.** Which points of interest should I look for along the route: viewpoints, cafes and restaurants, wineries, hot pools, museums, fuel stops, rest areas, stargazing spots?

8. **Overnight stays.** Where would you like to sleep each night (or should I suggest towns)? Will you be in a regular car or a campervan, and do you need hotels, motels or holiday parks?

9. **Car return.** Are you returning the rental car in Queenstown (one-way), and at what time? Is there a one-way fee or drop-off time I should plan around?
