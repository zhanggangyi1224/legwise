Before I dig into routes and options, here is a quick discovery batch so I can build a South Island trip that's actually yours. You've already told me the destination (South Island, self-drive), the rough timing (early March), that there are two of you, and the direction (Christchurch to Queenstown), so I won't re-ask those.

**What's the feeling you want to walk away with from the South Island, and what's drawing you to it now?** (For example: big-landscape awe, a hiking challenge, slow food-and-wine days, a romantic escape, a once-in-a-lifetime bucket-list run.)

Then the logistics:

1. **Home city and airport, and your nationality/passport.** Where are you flying in from, so I can price flights into Christchurch and out of Queenstown? And which passports do you hold, so I can check the NZeTA or visa rules for you?

2. **Exact dates and length.** "Early March" is a great window. How many days do you have on the ground, and what are the arrival and departure dates? Are they firm, or could they shift by a week or two if that meaningfully improves the trip?

3. **The two travelers.** Ages, any mobility limits (for example, comfortable with 3 to 5 hour hikes, or prefer short walks), dietary needs or allergies, and language comfort. Is one of you the planner and the other along for the ride, or do you both want input on the shape?

4. **Driving.** Will both of you drive, or just one? What licence do you hold (and is it in English, or will you need an International Driving Permit or translation)? Have you driven on the left before, and how many hours of driving a day are you comfortable with? Do you want a regular rental car or a campervan?

5. **Budget envelope.** A total, or per person per day, and what's in it: flights, car hire, accommodation, activities, food, everything? Which currency are you thinking in?

6. **Travel-style tier.** Budget / mid / upscale / luxury. (This decides between holiday parks and motels, boutique lodges, or the high-end lodges around Queenstown and Wānaka.)

7. **Pace.** Packed / balanced / chill. For a road trip this mostly means how many one-night stops you'll tolerate versus staying two or three nights in a base.

8. **Passions, ranked.** Your top 2 or 3 from: food, wine, art, history, nature, hiking, photography, stargazing, adrenaline (bungy, jet boat, skydive), rest, nightlife, design, wellness (hot pools), wildlife (penguins, seals, dolphins, whales), architecture, music.

9. **Non-negotiables, both sides.** What must you do or see (for example Milford Sound, Aoraki/Mount Cook, the glaciers on the West Coast, Lake Tekapo's dark-sky reserve, a Great Walk day section, Kaikōura whales)? And what must you avoid (long drive days, gravel roads, heights, crowds, cold water, early starts)?

10. **Route shape.** Christchurch to Queenstown direct via Tekapo and Mount Cook is about 6 hours of driving; going the long way round via Kaikōura, Nelson/Abel Tasman and the West Coast glaciers adds several days. Are you open to a longer loop if your days allow, or do you want the direct inland route done well?

11. **Hard constraints.** Work obligations during the trip, religious observances, allergies, how well you handle jet lag after a long flight in, fixed commitments (a wedding, a booked tour, a flight you already hold), anything else I should know.

Anything else floating around in your head about the trip? Answer all of it in one message and I'll come back with a shape.
