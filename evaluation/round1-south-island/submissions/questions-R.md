Sounds like a great trip. Before I build the day-by-day itinerary, a few questions so it fits the two of you:

1. **Dates and length:** What are your exact dates? When do you pick up the car in Christchurch, and when do you need to be in Queenstown (for example, for a flight out)? How many days do you have for the drive itself?

2. **Accommodation:** Have you booked anywhere yet? What style do you prefer (hotels, motels, holiday parks, Airbnb, lodges), and would you rather stay put for two or more nights in a few bases or move every night?

3. **Interests:** What do you most want from the trip? For example: hiking and walks, glaciers and lakes, wildlife (penguins, seals, whales, dolphins), stargazing, scenic drives, wineries and food, adventure activities (bungy, jet boat, skydiving, scenic flights), photography, hot pools.

4. **Activity level:** How active do you want to be? Easy strolls and viewpoints only, half-day walks of 2 to 4 hours, or full-day hikes (for example, Roys Peak or the Hooker Valley and beyond)?

5. **Must-dos vs nice-to-haves:** Is there anything you definitely want to include (for example, Aoraki/Mount Cook, Lake Tekapo, Milford Sound, the West Coast glaciers, Kaikōura, Wānaka, Dunedin and the Otago Peninsula)? Anything you'd skip?

6. **Food:** Any dietary restrictions or preferences I should plan around?

7. **Budget:** Roughly what's your budget per night for accommodation and for paid activities? Is something like a Milford Sound cruise or a glacier heli-hike in or out?

8. **Transport:** Have you booked a rental car or campervan yet, and what type? Who will drive, and are you both comfortable driving on the left on narrow, winding roads? How are you getting to Christchurch and leaving Queenstown?
