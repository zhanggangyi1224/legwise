# Before I plan: a few questions

Early March is a great time for the South Island (late summer, settled weather, fewer crowds than January). Before I route the trip and set up the booking timeline, I need a few details so the days aren't overpacked and the drive times are realistic. Short answers are fine, and "not sure" is a fine answer too.

## 1. Dates and length
- What are your exact dates (first and last day), or how many days do you have in total?
- What time do you land in Christchurch, and what time does your flight leave from Queenstown (or wherever you leave from)? This sets how much of the first and last days we can use.

## 2. Route shape
Christchurch to Queenstown can be done in very different ways. Which sounds closest?
- **A. Direct inland route** (Lake Tekapo, Mount Cook, then Queenstown). Shortest, about 6 to 7 hours of driving in total.
- **B. West Coast loop** (Arthur's Pass, Hokitika, Franz Josef / Fox glaciers, Haast Pass, Wānaka, Queenstown). Longer, more scenery, needs more days.
- **C. Big loop** including Kaikōura, Marlborough / Abel Tasman / Nelson in the north, then down the West Coast.
- **D. Not sure, recommend the best route for my number of days.**

Also: do you want to include Milford Sound (a long day trip from Queenstown or an overnight in Te Anau)?

## 3. The car and the drivers
- Have you already booked a rental car, or should I include that in the plan? Note that a one-way rental (Christchurch pick-up, Queenstown drop-off) usually has a one-way fee.
- Car or campervan?
- Will both of you drive, or only one? Do you both hold a full licence (and an English licence or an International Driving Permit)?
- How many hours of driving per day are you comfortable with? (For example: under 3 hours, 3 to 5 hours, or a long day now and then is fine.)

## 4. Pace and style
- What pace do you prefer? **Relaxed** (one main activity per day, plenty of free time), **Moderate**, or **Packed** (see as much as possible)?
- How much buffer time do you want for spontaneous stops and bad weather days? (I'd normally build in at least one flexible half-day.)

## 5. Interests
Which of these matter most to you? Pick any, and tell me your top two or three.
- Hiking (short walks only, or full-day hikes such as the Hooker Valley Track, Roys Peak, or Ben Lomond)
- Glaciers, lakes, and mountain scenery
- Wildlife (whale watching in Kaikōura, seals, penguins, dolphins)
- Stargazing (the Aoraki Mackenzie Dark Sky Reserve at Lake Tekapo)
- Adventure activities (bungy, jet boat, skydive, heli-hike)
- Wine and food (Central Otago, Gibbston Valley, Marlborough)
- Hot pools
- Photography
- Anything you already know you definitely want to do, or definitely want to skip?

## 6. Accommodation
- What kind of places do you want to stay in? (Hotels, motels, B&Bs, holiday homes, holiday parks, or a mix.)
- Rough budget per night for the two of you, in NZD or your own currency?
- Have you booked anything yet?
- Do you prefer to stay two nights in some places to reduce packing and unpacking?

## 7. Budget and bookings
- Rough total budget for the trip (excluding flights), or which areas you'd spend more on and where you'd rather save?
- Is there anything you have already booked (flights, car, tours, hotels)? Please paste the details so I can plan around them.
- How do you want to handle meals: mostly casual and self-catered, or a few special restaurant dinners worth booking ahead?

## 8. Anything else
- Any mobility, health, or dietary needs I should plan around?
- Is this a special occasion (a honeymoon, anniversary, or birthday)?
- Any concerns about driving in New Zealand (left-hand traffic, gravel or mountain roads, one-lane bridges)?

Once I have these, I'll give you an optimised day-by-day route with drive times, a booking timeline (what to book now and what can wait), buffer time, and backup plans for bad weather.
