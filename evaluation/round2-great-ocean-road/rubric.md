# Rubric (score each item 1, 0.5 or 0)

Context: the traveller first sent only "Can you plan a road trip from Melbourne to Adelaide along the Great Ocean Road over Christmas? There are three of us." Each run first wrote the questions it would ask (questions file), then received answers.md and produced report.md. Hidden answers are in answers.md.

## A. Questions before planning (questions file only)
1. Asks how many drivers and what licence.
2. Asks about the child or children (age, child seat) or who exactly the three travellers are.
3. Asks about hotel preferences: brand or loyalty program, room set-up for three, and budget.
4. Asks about daily driving limits, pace, or driving at dawn, dusk or after dark.
5. Asks what to avoid and what they're interested in.
6. Asks for flight times or arrival/departure details, including the last day.
7. Asks the output language/format, and the questions are quick to answer (choices or defaults), organised, not padded, and don't re-ask what was said.

## B. The plan (report.md)
8. Christmas is handled: what is closed or has reduced hours on 25 and 26 Dec is checked for the stops scheduled those days, and meals and fuel on Christmas Day are considered.
9. Avoid list respected: no helicopter or scenic flight scheduled, no walk over about 1 hour, no unsealed road used (or each is clearly excluded).
10. Every day ≤350 km and ≤ about 4 h driving, and driving ends at least 1 hour before that day's sunset, with no driving before sunrise (check at least three days; late-December sunset is about 20:40-20:50 in Victoria and about 20:25-20:30 in Adelaide).
11. Hotels: IHG used wherever IHG genuinely exists on the route, non-IHG nights explained, rooms fit 3, prices at or under A$350 or flagged (Christmas peak pricing considered).
12. Arrival day (land 08:30) and last day (15:00 end of sightseeing, car back at ADL by 16:00, 18:00 flight) handled realistically.
13. Rental logistics: one-way Melbourne to Adelaide fee, child seat, both adults named as drivers.
14. Facts that can change (opening hours, prices, road conditions) are sourced or clearly marked unverified; no invented places.
15. A booking list says what to book, by when (Christmas peak urgency), with links, and what can be bought on site.
16. Delivered as asked: a short English Markdown file, roughly one screen per day, phone-friendly, not padded. A long or PDF-style document fails this item.
