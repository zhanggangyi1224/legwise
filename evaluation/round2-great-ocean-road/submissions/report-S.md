### Day 1 · Wed 23 Dec · Airport to Geelong (easy arrival day)
- 08:30 land at MEL. About 09:45 collect the car and fit the child seat (allow 1 hour for bags and the rental desk).
- 10:00 to 11:00 drive to Geelong, about 80 km via the M80 and M1.
- **Geelong waterfront**, all flat and easy:
  - lunch;
  - the old carousel;
  - the Eastern Beach playground and its enclosed swimming area;
  - the painted Baywalk Bollards.
- 15:00 check in and rest after the early flight.
- **Buffer**: the afternoon is free. If you have energy, Torquay is 25 minutes away (beach and playground), but tomorrow covers it anyway.
- Sunset 20:46.

### Day 2 · Thu 24 Dec (Christmas Eve) · Great Ocean Road, east half
- 08:30 leave Geelong.
- **Bells Beach lookout**: sealed road, 10 minutes.
- **Anglesea**: wild kangaroos often graze on the golf course. Look from the roadside on Golf Links Road; don't walk onto the course.
- **Split Point Lighthouse, Aireys Inlet**: short walk around the outside. This is the lighthouse from the TV show "Round the Twist".
- **Memorial Arch at Eastern View**: photo stop.
- 12:00 **Lorne**: lunch, beach and playground. The beach is patrolled; swim between the flags.
- 14:30 **Kennett River koalas**: park in the town and walk the first few hundred metres of Grey River Road, looking up into the gums. **Don't drive up the road; it turns to gravel.**
- 16:30 arrive in Apollo Bay.
- **Before the shops close (shorter Christmas Eve hours)**:
  - fill the tank;
  - buy Christmas Day picnic food and snacks. Victorian supermarkets are **closed on 25 Dec**.
- Skip Cape Otway Lightstation: it is open Friday to Tuesday only, so it is closed on Thursday 24 Dec, and it is also closed on Christmas Day.
- Sunset 20:51.

### Day 3 · Fri 25 Dec (Christmas Day) · Shipwreck Coast
Everything today is an outdoor lookout that stays open on Christmas Day. Christmas morning is also one of the quietest times at the Apostles.
- 08:30 leave Apollo Bay.
- **Maits Rest**: 30-minute rainforest boardwalk loop.
- About 10:30 **Twelve Apostles**: boardwalk lookouts. The **Gibson Steps** (86 steps) go down to the beach; stay on the sand only at low tide.
- **Loch Ard Gorge**: short paths and a sheltered beach the kids love.
- About 12:30 Port Campbell: Christmas picnic on the foreshore, or a booked lunch.
- **London Bridge, The Grotto and Bay of Islands**: each is 5 to 15 minutes from the car park.
- About 15:30 **Tower Hill Reserve** near Warrnambool: emus, koalas and often kangaroos inside a volcanic crater. Drive the sealed loop and do short walks only. The visitor centre is probably closed today; the reserve is open.
- 17:00 arrive in Port Fairy.
- **Buffer**: if the Apostles car park is full, do Loch Ard Gorge first and come back.
- Sunset 20:56.

### Day 4 · Sat 26 Dec (Boxing Day) · Into South Australia
- Optional at 07:30: the **Griffiths Island** lighthouse loop in Port Fairy, flat, about 45 minutes. Wallabies are often seen.
- 09:00 drive to Mount Gambier, about 160 km, 1 h 50. **Before the border, eat or bin all fresh fruit and vegetables**; South Australia bans them because of fruit fly. Clocks go back 30 minutes.
- About 10:30 SA time, **Mount Gambier**:
  - the Blue Lake lookout (bright blue in summer);
  - the **Umpherston Sinkhole** sunken garden;
  - lunch.
- 13:30 drive to Robe via Millicent, about 130 km, 1 h 30.
- About 15:15 **Robe**: town beach and the Obelisk lookout on the sealed town road. **Avoid Little Dip Conservation Park; its roads are gravel.**
- **Buffer**: if anyone is tired, skip the Griffiths Island walk.
- Sunset 20:32 (SA time).

### Day 5 · Sun 27 Dec · Coorong to Adelaide (longest day, so break it up)
- 08:30 leave Robe.
- About 09:10 **Kingston SE**: the **Big Lobster ("Larry")**, a guaranteed hit with a 6-year-old.
- Drive the Princes Highway beside the **Coorong**. Use the sealed roadside stops only; don't take the side tracks.
- About 11:30 **Meningie**: lunch on the lakefront. Pelicans are usually there.
- 13:00 drive to Adelaide via Tailem Bend and the M1, about 150 km, 1 h 45.
- About 15:30 check in, then a short walk or a pool, and an early night.
- **Plan B** if 3 h 50 feels too long: sleep in Meningie (about 190 km from Robe) and drive about 175 km on Day 6 instead. That loses the second IHG night.
- Sunset 20:31.

### Day 6 · Mon 28 Dec · Wildlife morning, then fly
Today is a public holiday in South Australia, so expect cafe surcharges and holiday hours.
- 09:00 check out and drive to **Cleland Wildlife Park**, about 25 minutes. It is open 09:30 to 17:00 every day except Christmas Day.
- 09:30 to 12:00 at Cleland:
  - hand-feed kangaroos and wallabies;
  - see the koalas;
  - walk the easy paths.
- Optional: the Mount Lofty Summit lookout is 5 minutes away and the road is sealed.
- 12:30 **Hahndorf**: lunch and a stroll down the main street.
- 14:00 drive to the airport, about 40 km, 45 minutes. Refuel near the airport.
- **Return the car by 15:00**. That leaves a 1-hour buffer before the 16:00 deadline.
- Flight 18:00.

## Recommendations

### Book now (in priority order; Christmas stock is already thin)
1. **One-way rental car**: MEL on 23 Dec around 09:30, returned to ADL on 28 Dec by 16:00, with a **child seat**. Check:
   - the one-way fee;
   - that the ADL desk is open on the 28 Dec public holiday;
   - the no-gravel clause.
2. **Holiday Inn & Suites Geelong**, 23 Dec: a family room or two queen beds.
3. **Apollo Bay**, 24 Dec, and **Port Fairy**, 25 Dec. Coastal places sell out at Christmas, so book refundable rooms today.
4. **Robe**, 26 Dec: a motel or apartment for 3.
5. **Adelaide IHG**, 27 Dec: **Holiday Inn Express Adelaide City Centre** or **Crowne Plaza Adelaide**. Hotel Indigo Adelaide Markets is also IHG. The InterContinental may exceed A$350.
6. **Christmas Day meal**: book a lunch in Port Campbell or a dinner in Port Fairy if you want one. Otherwise bring the picnic.
7. Optional: a Cleland koala experience for 28 Dec. Session times are limited.

**Assumptions** (not confirmed with you): the hotel prices haven't been checked, so confirm each one is under A$350; you are happy with non-IHG places for 3 nights; nothing else is booked.
