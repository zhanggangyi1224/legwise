# Melbourne to Adelaide: Great Ocean Road at Christmas

**Trip:** Wed 23 Dec to Mon 28 Dec 2026, 5 nights, one-way, self-drive
**Travellers:** 2 adults (both driving) and 1 child aged 6 (needs a child seat)
**Main aim:** relaxed coastal driving with as much wildlife as possible for a 6-year-old (koalas, kangaroos, emus), short walks only, sealed roads only.

## The plan at a glance

| Day | Date | Route | Approx. km / drive | Sleep |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wed 23 | MEL Airport → Anglesea → Lorne → Kennett River → Apollo Bay | 200 km / 3h | Apollo Bay |
| 2 | Thu 24 | Apollo Bay → Twelve Apostles → Port Campbell → Warrnambool → Port Fairy | 190 km / 3h | Port Fairy |
| 3 | Fri 25 (Christmas) | Tower Hill → Port Fairy → Mount Gambier | 190 km / 2h40 | Mount Gambier |
| 4 | Sat 26 (SA holiday) | Mount Gambier → Beachport → Robe → Kingston SE | 180 km / 2h20 | Kingston SE |
| 5 | Sun 27 | Kingston SE → Coorong → Meningie → Hahndorf → Adelaide | 295 km / 3h45 | Adelaide (IHG) |
| 6 | Mon 28 (SA holiday) | Cleland → Mt Lofty → Glenelg → ADL car return by 16:00 | 55 km / 1h10 | (fly 18:00) |

Drive times are on the conservative side (a free routing engine, which runs slower than Google). Every day is under 350 km and about 4 hours of driving, and you are always parked at least 1 hour before sunset. Sunset is roughly 20:30–20:55 everywhere on this route, so in practice you finish driving by about 17:00. Sunrise is around 06:00, and no day starts before 08:30.

**Clocks:** when you cross into South Australia on Day 3, clocks go **back 30 minutes**. Victoria is on AEDT and SA on ACDT, and your phone switches automatically.

### Why this shape (the trade-offs)
- **Christmas Day is a short travel day, not a rest day.** On 25 Dec almost everything is shut anyway, and the roads are quiet. The stretch from Port Fairy to Adelaide is about 600 km. Fitting it into three days is the only way to keep each day under about 4 hours, with your last night in Adelaide and an easy final morning before the 18:00 flight. If Christmas were a no-drive day, Days 4 and 5 would each be roughly 4 hours or more (Port Fairy→Robe 290 km, then Robe→Adelaide 336 km, which comes out at about 4h10 in our routing). That breaks your limit and leaves no slack on flight day.
- **Kingston SE rather than Robe on night 4.** You still get Robe for lunch and the afternoon. Sleeping 44 km further on trims Day 5 to about 295 km instead of 340.
- **IHG: 1 of 5 nights.** IHG has no hotels between Melbourne Airport and Adelaide on this route. The old Crowne Plaza Torquay now trades as a Wyndham. Holiday Inn & Suites Geelong exists, but sleeping there on night 1 would push Day 2 well past 4 hours. The other nights are "anything clean", all non-IHG.

## Day 1 – Wed 23 Dec: Land soft, first koalas
**Theme:** an easy arrival day with one wildlife highlight · **Energy:** low

- **08:30** land at MEL. Pick up the car around 09:30 (allow +1h if you arrive on an international flight). Fit and check the child seat before leaving the car park.
- **09:30–11:15** drive to **Anglesea** (124 km). Stretch your legs on the riverbank and beach. Eastern grey kangaroos often graze on the Anglesea golf course, so look from the edge of the course.
- **12:00** lunch in **Lorne** (30 min on). The Swing Bridge Café by the Erskine River works well with a child: next to the footbridge and the beach. Check Christmas-week hours.
- **14:30–15:30** ⭐ **Anchor: Kennett River koala walk** (25 min past Lorne). Park at the Kafe Koala store and **walk** 1–2 km up Grey River Road, where koalas sit low in the gums. The road is **gravel**, so leave the car parked: your rental contract forbids unsealed roads. Allow 45–60 minutes return at a 6-year-old's pace.
- **16:15** Apollo Bay (23 km). Check in, then time on the foreshore playground and beach. Swim only between the red and yellow flags.
- **Dinner:** Apollo Bay harbour fish and chips on the grass by the water. It's casual and early, which suits jet lag.
- **Backup (rain or tired child):** skip Anglesea and Lorne and go straight to Kennett River, then the hotel.
- **Off the road by:** 19:50 (sunset 20:50). You're done by about 16:15.

## Day 2 – Thu 24 Dec (Christmas Eve): The Twelve Apostles
**Theme:** the famous coast · **Energy:** medium

- **09:00** leave Apollo Bay. The road over Lavers Hill is 90 km of **winding** rainforest road (about 1h30). If your child gets car-sick, give a light breakfast and seat them in the middle with a view ahead.
- **10:30–11:45** ⭐ **Anchor: Twelve Apostles boardwalk** (flat, about 20 min) plus **Gibson Steps** lookout. Parking and the lookouts are free; the new paid booking system applies only to the new visitor centre.
- **11:45–12:30** **Loch Ard Gorge** lookouts (5 min drive). The beach access was closed in 2026 because of cliff movement, so treat it as a lookout stop and check the signs.
- **12:45** lunch in **Port Campbell** (8 min). Choose a café or take fish and chips to the sheltered swimming beach.
- **14:00** quick stops at **London Bridge / Bay of Islands** lookouts as you head west (both just off the road, sealed).
- **15:30** **Warrnambool:** ⚠️ **fill the tank and do the Christmas shopping here.** Major supermarkets in Victoria are **closed on Christmas Day**. Buy breakfast, snacks and drinks for 25 Dec. Don't buy much fresh fruit: see the SA fruit-fly rule in the checklist.
- **Optional:** if the child needs a run, **Lake Pertobe** adventure playground in Warrnambool (free, about 45 min).
- **16:45** check in at Port Fairy (28 km).
- **17:30** **Griffiths Island lighthouse loop** (flat, about 45 min, from the town). You may see swamp wallabies, and the shearwater birds fly in at dusk. Walk there from the hotel if you can.
- **Backup (wind or rain on the cliffs):** stay longer at the Apostles visitor area, skip London Bridge, and arrive early at Port Fairy.
- **Off the road by:** 19:55 (sunset 20:55).

## Day 3 – Fri 25 Dec (Christmas): Emus for breakfast, then to SA
**Theme:** relaxed Christmas morning with one short drive · **Energy:** low to medium

- **08:30–10:00** ⭐ **Anchor: Tower Hill Wildlife Reserve** (16 km back east, 20 min). It sits inside a volcanic crater: emus wander next to the car park, koalas sit in the trees, and you may see kangaroos. Choose one short loop of about 30–45 minutes. The reserve is normally open every day, but the visitor centre is closed (renovation), so bring water. Toilets are open.
- **10:30–14:00** back in Port Fairy for **Christmas lunch.** **Book a Christmas Day sitting now** (see the booking list). If nothing is available, have a picnic on the Port Fairy foreshore with what you bought on the 24th.
- **14:30** drive to **Mount Gambier** (156 km, about 2h). **Clocks go back 30 min** at the border, so you arrive around 16:00 SA time.
- **16:30** **Cave Garden** (a sinkhole garden in the city centre, free, 15 min), then the **Blue Lake** lookout. December is when it turns its brightest cobalt blue.
- **Dinner:** almost everything is shut on Christmas night. Either book Christmas dinner at your hotel or motel when you book the room, or eat the leftovers from your Warrnambool shop.
- **Backup:** if you're tired after lunch, leave Port Fairy at 14:30 anyway, go straight to the hotel, and see the Blue Lake tomorrow morning.
- **Off the road by:** 19:30 (sunset 20:29 SA time).

## Day 4 – Sat 26 Dec (Boxing Day / SA public holiday): Sinkholes and a salt lake
**Theme:** a beach-hopping day · **Energy:** medium

- **09:00** ⭐ **Umpherston Sinkhole**, a sunken garden you walk down into (free, about 30 min). Children love it.
- **09:45** drive to **Beachport** via Millicent (85 km, about 1h05, sealed).
- **11:00–12:30** **Pool of Siloam**, a very salty lake on the edge of town where children float easily, plus Beachport's long jetty. Bring swimmers and a towel, and rinse off afterwards. Picnic or café lunch in Beachport.
- **13:00** drive to **Robe** on the Southern Ports Highway (50 km, about 40 min, sealed).
- **13:45–16:00** **Robe**, a heritage fishing town. Beach time on the sheltered town beach, the Obelisk lookout (stay on the sealed road and car park), and the lolly and ice-cream shops on Victoria Street. Expect it to be busy: Robe is SA's classic Christmas-holiday town.
- **16:00** drive to **Kingston SE** (44 km, 35 min). Take a photo with **Larry the Big Lobster**, then check in.
- **Note:** SA public holiday. Some cafés add a 10–15% surcharge and some close.
- **Backup (hot day over 35°C):** skip the Obelisk walk, have a longer swim at Robe, and arrive early in Kingston.
- **Off the road by:** 19:30 (sunset about 20:31).

## Day 5 – Sun 27 Dec: The Coorong to the Adelaide Hills
**Theme:** the longest drive, broken into three short legs · **Energy:** medium (most of it is sitting in the car)

- **08:30** leave Kingston on the Princes Highway through **Coorong National Park**.
- **09:30** stop at **Salt Creek** (about 70 km). There's a lagoon view and toilets. Stay on the highway: most Coorong side tracks are **gravel**, so skip them.
- **10:45** **Meningie** (75 km on). A break on the Lake Albert foreshore with a playground and pelicans often on the water.
- **11:15** drive to **Hahndorf** via Tailem Bend (124 km, about 1h30).
- **12:45** lunch in Hahndorf. The Hahndorf Inn is a German-style pub with a kids' menu, on the main street.
- **14:00–15:00** ⭐ **Anchor: Beerenberg Farm strawberry picking** (Hahndorf edge). The season runs November to April. Entry is A$8 per adult, free for children 12 and under, and berries are A$15/kg. The patch closes on very hot days, during fire warnings or once picked out, so **check their website after 09:00 that morning.**
- **15:30** drive to Adelaide city (26 km, 30 min) and check in at the **IHG hotel** (see bookings).
- **Backup (patch closed):** walk Hahndorf's main street and eat a strawberry ice-cream, or go straight to the hotel and its pool if it has one.
- **Off the road by:** 19:30 (sunset 20:31). You're parked by about 16:00.

## Day 6 – Mon 28 Dec (SA public holiday): Kangaroos, then fly
**Theme:** the best wildlife morning, then a calm airport run · **Energy:** medium

- **09:00** check out and drive to **Cleland Wildlife Park** (17 km, 22 min). It's open daily 09:30–17:00 and closes only on Christmas Day and days of catastrophic fire danger.
- **09:30–12:30** ⭐ **Anchor: Cleland.** Hand-feed free-roaming kangaroos and wallabies, and see emus and koalas. Book a **koala experience** ahead (Koala Close-up or Hold a Koala). Have lunch at the **Barking Gecko Café** inside the park.
- **12:45** **Mount Lofty Summit** lookout over Adelaide (5 min drive, sealed, 20 min stop).
- **13:30–15:00** **Glenelg** (about 35 min). Beach, foreshore playground and an ice-cream on Jetty Road.
- **15:00** refuel near the airport (Glenelg to ADL is about 10 min). **Return the car by 15:45**, with 15 minutes' buffer before the 16:00 deadline. Check in for the 18:00 flight.
- **Backup (catastrophic fire-danger day, when Cleland closes):** spend the morning in the Adelaide Botanic Garden, then go to Glenelg.

## Where to stay (book now: Christmas school holidays)

Rule for every night: one room for 3 (a family room or 2 queen beds), **under A$350 a night**, free cancellation if possible, and parking on site.

| Night | Town | Brand | What to look for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 23 Dec | Apollo Bay | non-IHG | Motel or apartment walking distance to the foreshore |
| 24 Dec | Port Fairy | non-IHG | In town, so you can walk to Griffiths Island and Christmas lunch |
| 25 Dec | Mount Gambier | non-IHG | Motel that **serves Christmas dinner** or has a kitchenette |
| 26 Dec | Kingston SE | non-IHG | Motel on the foreshore or highway (few options, so book first) |
| 27 Dec | Adelaide | **IHG** | See the three options below |

**Adelaide IHG: three options**
1. **Crowne Plaza Adelaide (recommended).** City centre on Hindmarsh Square, quieter than Hindley Street, and an easy run up to Cleland. Ask for 2 queens.
2. **Holiday Inn Express Adelaide City Centre.** Usually the cheapest and includes breakfast. It's on Hindley Street, Adelaide's nightlife strip, so expect noise on a holiday Sunday night.
3. **InterContinental Adelaide.** North Terrace on the riverbank, the nicest of the three, but likely **over A$350** at Christmas.
(Crowne Plaza Mawson Lakes lists family rooms, but it's 15 km north, which adds driving on both days. Use it only if the city options are full or over budget.)

Put your IHG One Rewards number on the booking.

## Checklist

**Before you go**
- [ ] Car booking confirms the child seat, both drivers, and that one-way to ADL is allowed
- [ ] Both driver licences (physical cards) and the booking confirmation in your bag
- [ ] Download offline maps for the whole route: mobile coverage drops out around Lavers Hill and the Coorong
- [ ] Save every hotel's phone number and check-in time
- [ ] Travel or car-hire excess insurance decision (A$0 excess cover is worth it on wildlife roads)

**On the road**
- [ ] **No unsealed roads.** If the map routes you onto gravel (Grey River Road, Coorong tracks), turn back
- [ ] **Off the road at least 1 hour before sunset** (cutoff about 19:30–19:55). Kangaroos are most active at dusk and dawn
- [ ] **Fill up in Warrnambool on 24 Dec.** Fuel and food are limited on Christmas Day
- [ ] **SA fruit-fly rule:** fresh fruit and some vegetables can't be taken from Victoria into SA. Eat them or bin them before the border on 25 Dec. There are bins at the border on the Princes Highway, and fines apply
- [ ] Clocks go back 30 min when you enter SA
- [ ] Swim only at flagged, patrolled beaches. Rips are common on this coast
- [ ] Check fire danger every morning: **VicEmergency** app (Victoria) and **Alert SA** (SA). On catastrophic days parks close; change your plans
- [ ] Return the car by 15:45 on 28 Dec

**Pack (late-December coast: 14–35°C, often windy)**
- Hats, SPF50 sunscreen, sunglasses; a warm layer and windproof jacket for the cliffs
- Swimmers, towels, a change of clothes for the Pool of Siloam
- Snacks, a water bottle each, a car-sickness kit for the Lavers Hill section
- Light walking shoes (every walk is under 1 hour)

**Emergency**
- 000 (police, fire, ambulance) · 112 from a mobile with no coverage
- Hospitals on the route: Warrnambool Base Hospital (Days 2–3), Mount Gambier Hospital (Days 3–4), Adelaide (Days 5–6)
- Car-hire roadside assistance number: save it at pick-up

**Budget note:** accommodation up to A$1,750 (5 × A$350), plus car hire, fuel (about 1,100 km), Cleland entry and koala experience, and meals. Add a **10–15% buffer** for holiday surcharges on 26 and 28 Dec.
