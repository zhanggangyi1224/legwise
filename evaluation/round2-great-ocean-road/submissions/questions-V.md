Happy to plan this. A few things would change the plan and I can't look them up, so before I start:

1. **Exact dates.** Which day do you leave Melbourne, and by when do you need to be in Adelaide? Are any days fixed, for example a Christmas Day lunch somewhere, or a flight home from Adelaide (if so, the flight time)?

2. **The car.** Your own car, or a rental? If it's a rental, have you checked it can be picked up in Melbourne and dropped off in Adelaide? One-way interstate hire often carries a fee, and some companies limit it over Christmas.

3. **Drivers.** Which of the three of you will drive, and does everyone have a full licence that's valid in Australia (overseas licences may need an English translation or an International Driving Permit)? Is anyone under 25? That affects rental rules and how much driving a day is comfortable.

4. **Daily limits.** Is there a most you'd want to drive in a day, in hours or km? Are you happy to drive after dark, or should every day finish in daylight? (I'd recommend daylight on the Great Ocean Road: it's winding and wildlife is out at dusk.)

5. **Must-sees and must-avoids.** Anything you definitely want on the way (for example the Twelve Apostles, Otway rainforest walks, koalas at Kennett River, Port Fairy, the Grampians, Mount Gambier's Blue Lake, Coonawarra wineries), or anything you'd rather skip?

6. **Where you sleep.** Any preference or budget for accommodation (hotels, motels, holiday parks, Airbnb), and do the three of you need one room or more? Christmas is peak season on the coast, so this matters for booking early.
