# Melbourne to Adelaide via the Great Ocean Road, 23 to 28 Dec 2026

## Overview

- **Who:** 2 adults (both drive) and 1 child aged 6 (needs a child seat). One room for all 3.
- **Route:** Melbourne Airport → Apollo Bay → Port Fairy (2 nights, Christmas) → Mount Gambier → Robe → Adelaide → Adelaide Airport. About 1,170 km in total, all on sealed roads.
- **Focus:** Coastal lookouts, wild koalas, kangaroos and emus, short easy walks, and beach time.
- **Your rules, and how the plan meets them:**
  - The longest day is about 340 km and about 4 h of driving.
  - Every day ends by about 16:30, well before the cutoff of 1 hour before sunset.
  - You never drive before sunrise.
  - No walk is longer than about 1 hour, and there are no gravel roads and no flights.
- **Hotels:** Only 1 of the 5 nights can be IHG. There are no IHG hotels between Melbourne and Adelaide, so the IHG night is the last one, in Adelaide. The other nights are a clean motel or apartment with a family room, up to A$350.
- **Time zone:** South Australia is **30 minutes behind** Victoria. Clocks go back at the border on 26 Dec.
- **Public holidays:** Fri 25 Dec. Sat 26 Dec. Mon 28 Dec is expected to be the substitute Boxing Day / Proclamation Day holiday. Expect holiday surcharges, and some shops closed or on short hours.

Sunset is about 20:50 to 21:00 in Victoria (AEDT) and about 20:30 in South Australia (ACDT). That puts your "off the road" cutoff at about 19:50 in Victoria and about 19:30 in South Australia. Sunrise is around 06:00. The cutoffs are not tight on any day.

## Day 1, Wed 23 Dec: Surf Coast to Apollo Bay

**Morning**
- **08:30** Land at MEL. Allow about 1 hour for bags and the rental desk. Check that the child seat is fitted before you leave.
- **09:45** Drive to Torquay on the M80 and M1, about 100 km and 1 h 10. Stop for coffee and the toilet.
- **11:15** Anglesea: kangaroos often graze at Anglesea Golf Club (Golf Links Rd), and you can see them from the edge of the course.
- **11:45** Aireys Inlet: Split Point Lighthouse, viewed from outside. Then the Memorial Arch at Eastern View for a photo.

**Afternoon**
- **12:30** Lunch in Lorne and a play on the foreshore. Teddy's Lookout is on a sealed road with a 5-minute walk.
- **14:30** **Kennett River koalas.** Park at Kafe Koala (Great Ocean Rd, Kennett River). **Walk** the first few hundred metres up Grey River Rd and look up into the gums. Grey River Rd is dirt, so **don't drive on it**. Allow 30 to 45 minutes. King parrots often land on people there.
- **15:30** Drive to Apollo Bay, about 23 km and 25 minutes. Check in by about 16:00.

**Evening**
- Walk the beach and harbour, then have an early fish and chips dinner.

**Prep tonight**
- Fill the tank in Apollo Bay. There is little fuel on the Lavers Hill stretch.

## Day 2, Thu 24 Dec (Christmas Eve): Rainforest, Twelve Apostles, Port Fairy

**Morning**
- **08:30** Leave Apollo Bay.
- **08:50** Mait's Rest rainforest boardwalk, about 800 m and 20 to 30 minutes, with giant ferns.
- *Optional, adds about 26 km and 35 minutes:* turn onto the sealed Lighthouse Rd and drive a few km to look for koalas in the roadside trees, then come back. The Cape Otway Lightstation itself is **closed Wed and Thu** (it opens Fri to Tue), so don't go in.
- **10:30** Gibson Steps: 86 steps down to the beach below the cliffs, about 20 minutes.
- **10:50** **Twelve Apostles** viewing platforms, reached by a tunnel from the car park, about 40 minutes. The car park is busiest from noon to 15:00, so arriving early helps.

**Afternoon**
- **11:45** Loch Ard Gorge: short paths with steps down to a sheltered cove, about 30 to 45 minutes.
- **12:45** Lunch in Port Campbell.
- **14:00** London Arch (formerly London Bridge) and the Bay of Islands lookouts, quick stops.
- **15:30** Warrnambool: **buy food for Christmas Day and fill the tank.** Supermarkets close on 25 Dec and may close early tonight.
- **16:30** Port Fairy, about 28 km and 25 minutes. Check in for 2 nights.

**Prep tonight**
- Confirm your Christmas lunch booking or picnic plan. Put out the swimmers, hats and sunscreen.

## Day 3, Fri 25 Dec (Christmas Day): Port Fairy, almost no driving

What is open today: beaches, Tower Hill reserve grounds and Griffiths Island (all outdoors). What is closed: most shops, supermarkets, many cafes, Cape Otway Lightstation, and the Tower Hill visitor centre (also closed for renovation).

**Morning**
- Open presents at a slow pace.
- **10:00** **Tower Hill Wildlife Reserve**, 15 km and 15 minutes east of Port Fairy on the Princes Hwy. The roads inside are sealed. Emus usually wander around the picnic area, and you have a good chance of koalas and kangaroos. Do one short track only (Lava Spit or Journey of Discovery, about 20 to 30 minutes). Bring a picnic morning tea.

**Afternoon**
- **12:30** Christmas lunch, booked in advance, or self-catered at your apartment.
- **15:00** Beach time at East Beach. Swim only between the flags if it is patrolled.

**Evening**
- **18:30** **Griffiths Island**: walk from town over the causeway to the lighthouse and back, about 50 to 60 minutes. There are often wallabies, and you will see shearwater burrows. This is on foot, with no driving.

**Prep tonight**
- Eat or bin all fresh fruit and vegetables before tomorrow, because of South Australia's fruit fly rules (see the checklist). Pack a lunch as a backup.

## Day 4, Sat 26 Dec: Into South Australia, Mount Gambier and Robe

**Morning**
- **09:00 (Victoria time)** Leave Port Fairy on the Princes Hwy via Heywood, about 170 km and 1 h 55. **Clocks go back 30 minutes at the border.** Use the fruit fly bins on the Princes Hwy near Mount Gambier.
- **10:25 (SA time)** **Blue Lake** lookouts. In December the lake is a vivid blue. Stay about 30 minutes.
- **11:15** **Umpherston Sinkhole**: a sunken garden with stairs down into it. It is free and always open. Stay about 30 minutes.

**Afternoon**
- **12:00** Lunch and fuel in Mount Gambier. Cave Gardens is in the town centre and takes 10 minutes.
- **13:30** Drive to Robe via Millicent, about 130 km and 1 h 25.
- **15:00** Check in at Robe. Then the town beach, the jetty and an ice cream.

**Evening**
- Robe is very busy over Christmas, so book dinner, or get takeaway early.

## Day 5, Sun 27 Dec: The Coorong to Adelaide

This is the longest day: about 340 km and 3 h 55 of driving, under your limits. It is split into three legs.

**Morning**
- **08:30** Leave Robe.
- **09:05** Kingston SE: a photo with "Larry" the Big Lobster, plus a toilet stop. About 15 minutes.
- **09:20** Drive the Princes Hwy along the Coorong to Meningie, about 145 km and 1 h 35. Stop at the sealed roadside rest area at Salt Creek for pelicans and birds. Skip the gravel side tracks to the lagoon.

**Afternoon**
- **11:30** Lunch and fuel at Meningie, with a lakefront playground on Lake Albert.
- **12:45** Drive via Tailem Bend and the M1 South Eastern Fwy to the Adelaide city centre, about 150 km and 1 h 45.
- **14:45** Check in at your **IHG hotel**. Have a rest or a swim. Late in the afternoon, walk the Botanic Garden or Rymill Park.

**Prep tonight**
- Pack for the flight. Your bags will stay in the car tomorrow, so keep valuables out of sight.

## Day 6, Mon 28 Dec: Adelaide Hills, then the airport

**Morning**
- **08:50** Check out. Drive 20 km and 25 minutes to **Cleland Wildlife Park** (365 Mount Lofty Summit Rd, Crafers). It opens 09:30, and the 28th is open.
  - Hand-feed kangaroos and wallabies in the walk-through areas.
  - See koalas up close. Cleland usually runs a koala encounter at set times, so book ahead.
  - Allow about 2 h 15.
- **11:45** Mount Lofty Summit lookout, 5 minutes away, with a view over Adelaide. Stay about 15 minutes.

**Afternoon**
- **12:30** Lunch in Hahndorf, a German-style village 20 minutes away.
- **14:30** Sightseeing ends. Drive to Adelaide Airport, about 40 km and 45 minutes, and refill the tank near the airport.
- **15:20** Return the car, which is due by 16:00. You have a 40-minute buffer.
- **18:00** Flight out of ADL.

## Booking list (book now, as Christmas is peak season)

1. **One-way rental car**, Melbourne Airport to Adelaide Airport.
   - Pick up 23 Dec at about 09:30. Return 28 Dec by 16:00.
   - Add a **child seat**: a booster or a harnessed seat, as required for ages 4 to 7. Add a **second driver** too.
   - Check the one-way fee and that kilometres are unlimited.
2. **Apollo Bay, 23 Dec (1 night):** a family room or 2-bedroom unit near the foreshore.
3. **Port Fairy, 24 and 25 Dec (2 nights):** an apartment **with a kitchen**, which is useful on Christmas Day.
4. **Robe, 26 Dec (1 night):** this town books out first, so book it first.
5. **Adelaide, 27 Dec (1 night, IHG):** use the IHG One Rewards member rate and ask for a room that sleeps 3 (two queens, or a queen plus a sofa bed). Either of these is about 25 minutes from Cleland and 20 minutes from the airport:
   - Holiday Inn Express Adelaide City Centre, 30 Blyth St
   - Crowne Plaza Adelaide, 27 Frome St
6. **Christmas lunch on 25 Dec**, at a Port Fairy or Warrnambool hotel or pub. These fill early. If you can't get one, self-cater.
7. **Cleland Wildlife Park tickets and koala encounter**, 28 Dec: book on clelandwildlifepark.sa.gov.au.

Expect A$350 a night to be tight in Apollo Bay and Robe at Christmas. Book early or widen the area a little: Marengo for Apollo Bay, Kingston SE for Robe.

## Checklist

- [ ] Both driver licences and a credit card for the rental bond.
- [ ] Offline maps saved on both phones: Geelong to Port Fairy, Port Fairy to Mount Gambier, and South East SA to Adelaide. Mobile signal drops in the Otways and on the Coorong.
- [ ] Fill the tank in Apollo Bay (23rd), Warrnambool (24th), Mount Gambier (26th) and Meningie (27th). Many small fuel stations close on 25 Dec.
- [ ] Buy Christmas Day food on 24 Dec. Supermarkets are closed on 25 Dec.
- [ ] **No fresh fruit or vegetables into South Australia.** Use the bins near Mount Gambier. Fines apply (see fruitfly.sa.gov.au).
- [ ] Clocks go back 30 minutes on 26 Dec. The car return (16:00) and the flight (18:00) are in SA time.
- [ ] Gear: strong sunscreen, hats, swimmers, and a warm layer, because the coast can be windy and 15°C even in December.
- [ ] Car sickness: the road from Lorne to Apollo Bay is very winding. Bring tablets, snacks and water for your child.
- [ ] On a **Catastrophic fire danger** day, parks such as Cleland close and you should change plans. Check VicEmergency (Victoria) and Alert SA (South Australia) each morning.
- [ ] Before sunset, watch for kangaroos and wombats near the road, especially on the Otways and Coorong sections.
- [ ] Return the car with a full tank, and photograph it at pick-up and return.
