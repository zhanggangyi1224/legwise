# Melbourne to Adelaide via the Great Ocean Road
**Wed 23 to Mon 28 Dec 2026 · 2 adults + 1 child (6) · 5 nights · about 1,095 km**

## Verdict
**The plan works.** Every day stays within your limits (350 km, about 4 hours of driving, off the road at least 1 hour before sunset, no driving before sunrise), and you have the car back at Adelaide Airport about 75 minutes before the 16:00 deadline. Nothing is booked yet, and a few opening hours and prices still need checking (listed at the end).

**What to do first:** book the one-way rental with a child seat, then the Port Campbell (24 Dec) and Mount Gambier (25 Dec) rooms. These are the ones most likely to sell out over Christmas.

**Two things I changed while checking**
- **Night 4 is in Kingston SE, not Robe.** With a Robe night, the last drive into Adelaide (27 Dec) came to 4h17 of driving, over your 4-hour limit. You still spend 2 hours in Robe on 26 Dec, and 27 Dec drops to 3h43.
- **15-minute stretch stop at Dartmoor on Christmas Day.** Without it you'd drive just over 2 hours without a break.

**IHG:** you get 2 of the 5 nights (Geelong and Adelaide). I found no IHG hotel between Geelong and Adelaide on this route, so a third IHG night isn't possible without breaking your daily limits.

**Tight days:** 24 Dec (3h33) and 25 Dec (3h56) are close to your 4-hour limit. These are router times plus a 10% buffer, and summer traffic on the Great Ocean Road can be slower. If you're running late, skip the optional stops marked *(skippable)*.

Times are local. **South Australia's clocks are 30 minutes behind Victoria's**, and you cross the border on 25 Dec. Sunset is about 20:30 to 20:50 every day, so your "off the road by" time is about 19:30 to 19:50.

## Day 1 · Wed 23 Dec · Melbourne Airport → Geelong
**83 km · 1h17 driving · off the road by 19:45**
- 08:30 land. Collect the car and fit the child seat. **Leave about 09:45.**
- 10:25–14:25 **Werribee Open Range Zoo** (38 km). Safari bus: lions, giraffes, rhinos. Lunch at the zoo. **Tickets must be pre-booked online.**
- 15:10 **Holiday Inn & Suites Geelong** (IHG) (44 km). Easy evening: the waterfront carousel and the Eastern Beach playground, on foot.

## Day 2 · Thu 24 Dec (Christmas Eve) · Geelong → Port Campbell, the Great Ocean Road
**209 km · 3h33 driving · off the road by 19:50**
- **08:30 leave.** Fill the tank in Geelong.
- 09:25 **Split Point Lighthouse**, Aireys Inlet (20 min, lookout)
- 10:10 **Lorne** foreshore, beach and playground (30 min)
- 11:05 **Kennett River koalas** (45 min). Park at the café or caravan park and **walk** a few hundred metres up Grey River Road to look for koalas in the trees. **Don't drive Grey River Road: it's gravel.**
- 12:20 **Apollo Bay**, lunch on the foreshore (1 h)
- 13:35 **Maits Rest** rainforest boardwalk (30 min loop, easy)
- 15:15 **Gibson Steps**, beach at the foot of the cliffs (25 min)
- 15:40 **Twelve Apostles** viewing platforms (40 min)
- 16:25 **Loch Ard Gorge**, short paths to the lookouts and the beach (40 min)
- 17:15 **Port Campbell**, overnight. Optional: drive back 12 km for the Apostles at golden hour (about 19:00) and be back before 19:50.
- **Buy Christmas Day food today.** Victorian supermarkets close on 25 Dec. Don't buy more fresh fruit than you'll eat before the SA border (see the checklist).

## Day 3 · Fri 25 Dec (Christmas Day) · Port Campbell → Mount Gambier
**263 km · 3h56 driving · off the road by 19:30 (SA time)**
- **09:00 leave.**
- 09:05 **London Bridge** lookout (20 min) *(skippable)*
- 09:40 **Bay of Islands** lookout (15 min) *(skippable)*
- 11:00–12:10 **Tower Hill Wildlife Reserve**, a volcanic crater with free-roaming emus, koalas and kangaroos. Picnic. *Christmas Day gate hours aren't confirmed, so check (see the unknowns list).*
- 12:35–14:05 **Port Fairy**, Griffiths Island walk to the lighthouse (about 40 min return, flat; wallabies and shearwater burrows). Park on the sealed side and walk the causeway.
- 15:50 **Dartmoor** rest stop with carved-tree sculptures (15 min). **Use the fruit quarantine bins before the border.**
- **Clocks go back 30 minutes at the border.**
- 16:25 **Mount Gambier** (SA time), overnight. **Most restaurants are closed today, so pre-book Christmas dinner** or eat from what you brought.

## Day 4 · Sat 26 Dec (SA public holiday) · Mount Gambier → Kingston SE
**194 km · 2h35 driving · off the road by 19:30**
- **09:30 leave.** Fill the tank (Mount Gambier has the most choice).
- 09:35 **Blue Lake** lookouts (30 min). The lake is at its bluest in December.
- 10:15 **Umpherston Sinkhole**, a sunken garden with stairs down (45 min). Usually open 10:00–15:00.
- 12:15 **Beachport**, long jetty and beach, lunch (1h15)
- 14:15–16:15 **Robe**: town beach, playground, ice cream, the Obelisk lookout from town. Don't drive into Little Dip Conservation Park or onto Long Beach: the tracks are gravel or sand.
- 16:50 **Kingston SE**, overnight. **Larry the Lobster** (giant lobster) is a hit with kids.

## Day 5 · Sun 27 Dec · Kingston SE → Adelaide
**294 km · 3h43 driving · off the road by 19:30**
- **09:00 leave.** Fill the tank in Kingston SE. Coorong lagoons and pelicans along the highway. Stay on the Princes Highway: the Coorong side tracks (for example 42 Mile Crossing) are unsealed.
- 10:55–11:55 **Meningie** lakefront, playground and lunch (1 h)
- 13:35–14:35 **Hahndorf**, a German village with lollies, bakeries and the main street (1 h)
- 15:05 **Holiday Inn Express Adelaide City Centre** (IHG). Free evening.

## Day 6 · Mon 28 Dec (SA public holiday) · Adelaide → airport
**51 km · 1h11 driving · car due by 16:00, flight 18:00**
- **09:10 leave.**
- 09:40–12:10 **Cleland Wildlife Park**: walk among kangaroos you can hand-feed, plus koalas. Open 09:30–17:00, closed only on Christmas Day and on catastrophic fire-danger days.
- 12:20 **Mount Lofty Summit** lookout over Adelaide (30 min)
- 13:20–14:35 **Glenelg**: beach, jetty and lunch. **Fill the tank nearby.**
- **14:45 return the car at Adelaide Airport.** That's 75 minutes before the 16:00 deadline.

## Book or confirm next (in this order)
1. **One-way rental, Melbourne Airport to Adelaide Airport, with a child seat for a 6-year-old.** Christmas fleets sell out. Get the one-way fee in writing, confirm the Adelaide Airport desk takes returns on the 28 Dec public holiday, and ask for the real fuel range (I assumed 550 km; the longest gap between planned fills is about 300 km).
2. **Port Campbell, 24 Dec.** A small town on Christmas Eve. Options: Port Campbell Motor Inn (family rooms) or the Portside Motel family apartment.
3. **Mount Gambier, 25 Dec, plus Christmas dinner.** Options: Motel Mount Gambier family suite, the Presidential Motel family room, or the Mount Gambier International Motel (2 queens).
4. **Kingston SE, 26 Dec.** Kingston Ocean View Motel family room (1 queen + 2 singles).
5. **Holiday Inn & Suites Geelong, 23 Dec**, and **Holiday Inn Express Adelaide City Centre, 27 Dec** (or Crowne Plaza Adelaide). Ask for a room that sleeps 3. Check each rate against your A$350 cap; I couldn't confirm any Christmas prices.
6. **Werribee Open Range Zoo tickets** for 23 Dec (online only).

## Checklist
- **Fruit fly rules:** you can't take fresh fruit or fruiting vegetables from Victoria into SA. Eat them or bin them before the border; there are random roadblocks and on-the-spot fines.
- **Change your watches** to SA time on 25 Dec (30 minutes back).
- **Christmas food:** buy on 24 Dec; expect most shops and cafés to be shut on 25 Dec and many on 26 Dec.
- **Fuel:** fill up at Geelong (24 Dec), Mount Gambier (26 Dec), Kingston SE (27 Dec) and Glenelg (28 Dec).
- **No gravel:** skip Grey River Road, Little Dip, Long Beach and the Coorong tracks. Everything else in the plan is on highways and town roads.
- **Walks:** none is longer than about 45 minutes. No flights.
- **Each morning:** check road closures and fire danger on VicTraffic and VicEmergency (Victoria) or Traffic SA and Alert SA. On a total fire ban or catastrophic day, parks may close.

## Still unknown, and how to find out
- **Tower Hill on Christmas Day:** gate hours, and whether the inside loop road is sealed. Call the reserve or check Parks Victoria. If it's closed, spend longer at Port Fairy.
- **Umpherston Sinkhole hours** over Christmas 2026: the only notice I found is from 2024. Check Mount Gambier council's Christmas hours page in December.
- **Werribee Zoo hours on 23 Dec:** check Zoos Victoria when you book.
- **Room prices and bed layouts:** I found no Christmas 2026 rates. Confirm 3 beds and the price under A$350 when booking.
- **Great Ocean Road works:** landslip works near Kennett River and barrier works near the Twelve Apostles were scheduled for 2026, and I found no December closures published. Check VicTraffic a few days before.
- I ran out of web searches before checking a few minor items. None of them changes the route.

*How this was checked:* distances and times come from OpenStreetMap routing plus a 10% buffer, and sunset times are calculated for each day and town. The check can't see live closures, bushfires, holiday traffic or how tired you are, so treat the tight days as tight.
