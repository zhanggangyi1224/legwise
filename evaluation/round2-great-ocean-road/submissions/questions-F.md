Happy to plan this. A Melbourne to Adelaide trip along the Great Ocean Road over Christmas is a great idea. Before I build a day-by-day itinerary, I need a few details. Short answers are fine, and you can skip anything you don't mind about.

**1. Dates and duration**
- Which day do you leave Melbourne and which day do you need to be in Adelaide (or home)?
- Roughly how many days do you have for the drive itself?
- Where do you want to be on Christmas Day (25 Dec) and Boxing Day (26 Dec)? Many restaurants, shops and some attractions close or change hours then, so I'll plan around it.

**2. Accommodation**
- Have you booked anywhere yet? If so, where and which nights?
- What kind of places do you like: hotels/motels, holiday rentals, caravan parks or cabins, camping?
- Room setup for the three of you: one room or apartment together, or separate rooms?

**3. Interests**
What do you most want from the trip? For example: coastal scenery and lookouts (Twelve Apostles etc.), hiking, beaches and swimming, surfing, wildlife (koalas, kangaroos, penguins), food and wine (e.g. Coonawarra, McLaren Vale), towns and history, photography, or a relaxed beach Christmas.

**4. Activity level**
- How active is the group? Easy walks only, moderate half-day hikes, or bigger hikes?
- How much driving per day feels OK? (Relaxed: 2 to 3 hours; moderate: 4 to 5 hours; long: 6 or more.)

**5. Must-dos and nice-to-haves**
- Is there anything you definitely want to see or do on the way?
- Anything you'd rather skip?

**6. Food**
- Any dietary restrictions or preferences in the group?
- Do you want a special Christmas lunch or dinner booked, or would you rather keep it simple (picnic, self-catered)?

**7. Budget**
- Roughly what budget are you aiming for (total or per night for accommodation), and are you happy to pay for paid experiences like a helicopter flight over the Twelve Apostles?

**8. Transport**
- What will you be driving: your own car, a rental, or a campervan? If it's a rental, where are you picking it up and returning it (one-way Melbourne to Adelaide)?
- How many of the three of you can drive?
- How are you getting home from Adelaide (flight, drive back, train)?
