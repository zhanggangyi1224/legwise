# Melbourne to Adelaide via the Great Ocean Road
**23 to 28 Dec 2026 · 2 adults + 1 child (6) · 5 nights · about 1,100 km**

Nothing is booked yet except your flights. This plan covers the driving part only.

**Your rules, and how the plan meets them**
- **Distance:** no day is over 350 km. The longest is 27 Dec at 296 km.
- **Driving time:** no day is over about 3.5 hours of actual driving.
- **Sunset:** you are parked every day by 18:00 at the latest, at least 2.5 hours before sunset. See the off-road time on each day.
- **Roads:** all sealed. Every walk is under about 1 hour. No scenic flights.

**How I estimated the drives**
- I took road distances from OpenStreetMap routing and added 10% for buffer.
- Times are local. South Australia (SA) is **30 minutes behind** Victoria. You change at the border on 25 Dec.

| Day | Route | km | Drive | Night |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wed 23 | Melbourne Airport → Apollo Bay | 200 | ~3 h | Apollo Bay |
| Thu 24 | Apollo Bay → Twelve Apostles → Warrnambool | 195 | ~3.5 h | Warrnambool |
| Fri 25 (Christmas) | Warrnambool → Port Fairy → Mount Gambier | 200 | ~3 h | Mount Gambier |
| Sat 26 | Mount Gambier → Beachport → Robe → Kingston SE | 185 | ~2.5 h | Kingston SE |
| Sun 27 | Kingston SE → Coorong → Adelaide | 296 | ~3.5 h | Adelaide (IHG) |
| Mon 28 | Adelaide Hills → Adelaide Airport | 70 | ~1.3 h | car back by 16:00 |

**Why I did not end the route in Robe:** sleeping in Robe on 26 Dec would make 27 Dec 339 km and about 4 to 4.5 hours of driving. That is over your limit, so you sleep in Kingston SE (43 km further on) instead.

## Day 1 · Wed 23 Dec · Airport → Apollo Bay
**200 km, about 3 h of driving. Sunset 20:50. Be off the road by 19:50.**

- **08:30** Land. Collect the car with the child seat and fit it before leaving.
- **~10:00** Leave. Take the M80 and M1 past Geelong, then on to Torquay (105 km, about 1 h 35 min).
- **11:35–12:35** Torquay: lunch, and the playground on the foreshore.
- **13:10–13:50** Split Point Lighthouse, Aireys Inlet: short paths and coastal lookouts.
- **14:00** Stop for a photo at the Memorial Arch at Eastern View.
- **14:35–15:20** Lorne: the beach, the pier and the foreshore playground.
- **15:45–16:30** Kennett River: spot koalas in the trees, and see the parrots.
  - **Park on the Great Ocean Road and walk up Grey River Road. Do not drive it, because it becomes gravel.**
- **~16:55** Arrive in Apollo Bay. You have a free evening on the beach.

**Tip:** if you are tired after the flight, stay in Lorne instead (about 175 km). Day 2 then becomes about 45 km longer.

## Day 2 · Thu 24 Dec (Christmas Eve) · Apollo Bay → Warrnambool
**195 km, about 3.5 h of driving. Sunset 20:54. Be off the road by 19:50.**

- **08:30** Leave.
- **08:50–09:20** Maits Rest: a 30-minute rainforest boardwalk loop.
- **09:30** Lighthouse Road (sealed): look for koalas in the gums. Only stop where you can pull fully off the road.
- **09:45–11:30** Cape Otway Lightstation, with an early lunch at its café.
  - **Check it opens on a Thursday.** Some of its pages list Friday to Tuesday only. If it is shut, skip it and save about 1.5 hours.
- **12:50–13:20** Gibson Steps: 86 steps down to the beach.
- **13:25–14:15** Twelve Apostles. The visitor area is being rebuilt, so follow the signs for parking.
- **14:20–15:10** Loch Ard Gorge: easy paths.
- **15:25–15:55** Port Campbell: ice cream and the beach.
- **16:05** London Arch.
- **16:50** Bay of Islands.
- **~17:45** Arrive in Warrnambool. The Lake Pertobe playground is good for the child.

**Before the shops close tonight:**
- Fill the tank.
- Buy food for Christmas Day: breakfast and a picnic lunch. Supermarkets are closed on 25 Dec.

## Day 3 · Fri 25 Dec (Christmas Day) · Warrnambool → Mount Gambier
**200 km, about 3 h of driving. Sunset 20:29 SA time. Be off the road by 19:30.**

Everything today is outdoors and open-air, which suits Christmas Day.

- **09:30** Leave.
- **09:50–11:20** Tower Hill Wildlife Reserve.
  - You often see emus, koalas and kangaroos.
  - The Lava Tongue boardwalk takes about 20 minutes.
  - The visitor centre may be closed today.
  - Use only the sealed road into the reserve.
- **11:45–13:30** Port Fairy.
  - Walk across Griffiths Island to the lighthouse and back. It is flat and takes 45 to 60 minutes. You may see wallabies.
  - Have your picnic lunch.
- **13:30** Drive to Mount Gambier on the A1 (157 km, about 2 h 15 min). Set your clocks back 30 minutes at the border.
- **~15:15 (SA time)** Umpherston Sinkhole: a sunken garden with stairs down into it. It is free and open all day.
- **16:30** Blue Lake lookouts. The lake is at its bluest in summer.
- **Evening:** have Christmas dinner at your hotel or another restaurant, **booked in advance**.

**Fruit rule:** finish your fresh fruit and vegetables before crossing into South Australia. SA bans bringing them in, because of fruit fly.

## Day 4 · Sat 26 Dec (SA public holiday) · Mount Gambier → Kingston SE
**185 km, about 2.5 h of driving. Sunset 20:31. Be off the road by 19:30.**

- **09:30** Leave on the Princes Highway.
- **10:00** Tantanoola Caves: a guided tour of 30 to 40 minutes. The cave is easy for children.
  - Tours run every 30 minutes from 10:00 to 14:30. Booking is recommended.
- **11:00** Go to Millicent, then take the B101 Southern Ports Highway to Beachport.
  - **Ignore any sat-nav shortcut through Mount Burr.** It uses minor roads that may be unsealed.
- **11:45–13:00** Beachport: walk the long jetty and have lunch.
- **13:40–15:30** Robe: the town beach, the lolly shop and an ice cream.
  - **Do not drive onto Long Beach.** Driving on sand breaks your rental contract.
- **~16:10** Kingston SE: Larry the Big Lobster, the jetty and the foreshore playground.
- Fill the tank this evening.

## Day 5 · Sun 27 Dec · Kingston SE → Adelaide
**296 km, about 3.5 h of driving. Sunset 20:30. Be off the road by 19:30.**

- **09:00** Leave on the B1 Princes Highway. It runs along the Coorong lagoon.
- **10:10** Salt Creek: a toilet and a stretch.
  - Skip the Coorong Loop Road and the beach tracks. They are unsealed.
  - Jack Point Pelican Observatory is optional, only if its access road is clearly sealed.
- **11:15–12:30** Meningie lakefront: pelicans, a playground and lunch.
- **12:30** Drive via Tailem Bend and the M1 to the city (150 km, about 2 h).
- **~14:45** Check in. The afternoon is free: the hotel pool, the Botanic Garden, or the free tram to Glenelg beach. You do not need the car for any of these.

## Day 6 · Mon 28 Dec (SA public holiday) · Adelaide Hills → Airport
**70 km, about 1.3 h of driving. Car due back at 16:00. Flight at 18:00.**

- **08:50** Check out.
- **09:30–12:00** Cleland Wildlife Park.
  - You can feed kangaroos and get close to koalas.
  - It opens at 09:30. Get there at opening, because it is a public holiday.
  - Book the koala session. It may be cancelled if it is over 32 °C.
- **12:10–12:40** Mount Lofty Summit lookout.
- **13:00–14:30** Hahndorf: lunch and the main street.
- **14:30** Drive to Adelaide Airport (36 km, about 40 minutes). Fill the tank near the airport.
- **~15:15** Return the car, 45 minutes before your 16:00 deadline.

## Booking list (do these now, because it is Christmas peak)

1. **One-way rental car**
   - Pick-up: Melbourne Airport, 23 Dec at 09:30. Drop-off: Adelaide Airport, 28 Dec at 16:00.
   - Ask for a booster or child seat for a 6-year-old, both adults listed as drivers, and the one-way fee shown in the price.
2. **Hotels: one room for three, two queens or a family room, up to A$350 per night**
   - **23 Dec, Apollo Bay:** motel or apartment.
   - **24 Dec, Warrnambool:** motel.
   - **25 Dec, Mount Gambier:** pick one with a restaurant open on Christmas.
   - **26 Dec, Kingston SE:** motel or a family cabin. There are few rooms, so book this first.
   - **27 Dec, Adelaide (IHG):** Crowne Plaza Adelaide or Holiday Inn Express Adelaide City Centre. Hotel Indigo Adelaide Markets is also IHG. Check the room fits 3 within budget, and ask about car parking.
3. **Christmas dinner, 25 Dec, Mount Gambier:** book a table, or choose a room with a kitchen.
4. **Tantanoola Caves tour, 26 Dec, about 10:00:** book through Parks SA.
5. **Cleland Wildlife Park, 28 Dec:** entry plus a koala session.
6. **Optional:** Cape Otway Lightstation (24 Dec) and a Split Point Lighthouse tour (23 Dec).

**Why only one IHG night:**
- I found no IHG hotel between Geelong and Adelaide. Holiday Inn & Suites Geelong exists.
- A Geelong night on 23 Dec would push one later day over 350 km. For example, Mount Gambier to Adelaide is about 435 km.

## Checklist

- [ ] **Fill the tank every evening.** This is most important on 24 Dec in Warrnambool and 26 Dec in Kingston SE.
- [ ] **Buy food on 24 Dec** for the whole of 25 Dec.
- [ ] **Eat or bin fresh fruit and vegetables** before entering SA on 25 Dec.
- [ ] **Change your clocks** 30 minutes back at the SA border on 25 Dec.
- [ ] **Be off the road by the time shown each day**, 1 hour or more before sunset, because of kangaroos. Never drive before sunrise, which is about 05:45–06:05.
- [ ] **Sealed roads only.** Do not drive on Grey River Road at Kennett River, the Coorong Loop Road or 4WD tracks, or any beach.
- [ ] **Check fire and weather each morning** (VicEmergency or Alert SA). Parks and Cleland close on catastrophic fire-danger days.
- [ ] **Swim only at patrolled beaches**, between the flags.
- [ ] **Bring car-sickness remedies** for the winding Great Ocean Road (days 1 and 2), and swap drivers every hour or so.
- [ ] **Check the opening times again in the week before you go:** Cape Otway Lightstation, Tower Hill visitor centre, Tantanoola Caves.
- [ ] **Public holidays:** 25 Dec, 26 Dec, and 28 Dec in SA. Expect crowds and shorter hours.
