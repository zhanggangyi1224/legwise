# Melbourne to Adelaide by the Great Ocean Road
**Wed 23 to Mon 28 Dec 2026 · 2 adults and 1 child (6) · 5 nights · one-way hire car**

## Overview

| Wed 23 | Melbourne Airport to Apollo Bay | ~205 km | ~3 h 15 | Apollo Bay |
| Thu 24 | Apollo Bay to Port Fairy | ~215 km | ~3 h 30 | Port Fairy |

- Every day stays under your limits of 350 km and about 4 hours of driving.
- Distances and times come from a routing engine and include the detours listed. Real times are usually a little faster.
- Every road in this plan is sealed. Places where a sat nav might send you onto gravel are marked ⚠.

**Latest time to be off the road** (1 hour before sunset; sunrise is around 06:00, so mornings are never a problem):

| Date | Place | Sunset | Off the road by |

**Why the trip ends in Kingston SE, not Robe:** Robe to Adelaide is 337 km and about 4 hours 10 minutes, which is right at both your limits. Instead you visit Robe on the afternoon of 26 Dec and sleep 43 km further on.

## Day 1 · Wed 23 Dec · Airport to Apollo Bay (~205 km)
- **08:30** Land at Melbourne Airport. **About 09:30**, collect the car. Check the child seat is fitted and both drivers are on the contract.
- **10:00** Drive to **Torquay** (~1 h 20). Morning tea and a run on the beach.
- **Aireys Inlet:** view Split Point Lighthouse from the outside lookout. Photo stop at the **Memorial Arch** at Eastern View.
- **Lorne** (about 13:00): lunch, the foreshore playground and the swing bridge.
- **Kennett River** (about 14:45): koalas in the gum trees. Park by the café and **walk** 200 to 300 m up Grey River Road. Look for king parrots too. ⚠ Don't drive up Grey River Road, because it turns to gravel.
- **About 16:00** Arrive in **Apollo Bay**. Beach, harbour and an early dinner. Off the road by 19:50.

## Day 2 · Thu 24 Dec (Christmas Eve) · Apollo Bay to Port Fairy (~215 km)
- **08:30** Visit **Maits Rest**: an 800 m rainforest boardwalk loop (about 30 min).
- **Cape Otway Lighthouse Road:** drive the first ~5 km slowly to spot koalas, then turn back. The lightstation is closed on Thursdays and the tower is closed for works until January 2027, so don't go to the end.
- **About 11:15** Visit the **Twelve Apostles**: an easy boardwalk from the visitor car park, about 1 hour. The car park is busiest around midday, so park, go and enjoy it anyway.
- **Loch Ard Gorge:** short paths and a sheltered beach. Then lunch in **Port Campbell**.
- **London Bridge** and the **Bay of Islands** lookouts. Each is a 5-minute walk from the car.
- **About 16:00** Stop in **Warrnambool** for Christmas shopping and **fuel**:
  - Buy breakfast for 25 Dec and a picnic. Victorian supermarkets are closed on Christmas Day.
  - Buy only the fresh fruit and vegetables you'll eat by midday on the 25th. You can't take them into South Australia (see the checklist).
- **About 17:15** Visit **Tower Hill Wildlife Reserve**, a sealed road into a volcanic crater:
  - Emus, koalas, and often kangaroos.
  - Take a short loop walk of under 1 hour.
  - The reserve itself is always open. The visitor centre may be closed, but you don't need it.
- **About 18:30** Arrive in **Port Fairy**. Off the road by 19:55.

## Day 3 · Fri 25 Dec (Christmas Day) · Port Fairy to Mount Gambier (~180 km)
Most things are shut today. This day uses free outdoor sights that are always open, plus one booked meal.
- Presents in the morning, then a **Griffiths Island** walk. It's flat: go to the lighthouse and back in about 45 minutes, and look out for wallabies.
- **12:00** **Christmas lunch, booked** in Port Fairy. Pubs and hotels there usually run a set Christmas menu.
- **14:00** Leave for **Portland** (~1 h), then **Cape Bridgewater** (20 min more): a beach and short lookouts.
- **Before the border:** eat or bin all fresh fruit and vegetables (the border is about 40 min after Cape Bridgewater). **Set your clocks back 30 minutes.**
- **About 17:15 ACDT** Arrive in **Mount Gambier**. If there's time, visit **Umpherston Sinkhole**, a free sunken garden. Be off the road by 19:30.
- Dinner: Christmas leftovers, or a hotel restaurant if you can book one. Expect very few cafés to be open.

## Day 4 · Sat 26 Dec (Boxing Day, a public holiday in SA) · Mount Gambier to Kingston SE (~205 km)
- **09:00** Visit **Blue Lake**. It's at its bluest in summer. Drive around the rim and stop at the lookouts. Visit Umpherston Sinkhole now if you missed it yesterday.
- **10:30** Drive through Millicent to **Beachport** (~1 h 20): the long jetty, and the **Pool of Siloam**, a very salty lake where kids float easily. Lunch here.
- **14:00** Drive to **Robe** (~40 min): the Obelisk lookout, the town beach and ice cream.
  - ⚠ Skip Little Dip Conservation Park. Its tracks are sand and gravel.
  - ⚠ Don't drive onto Long Beach. Park at the beach entrance instead.
- **About 17:00** Drive to **Kingston SE** (~35 min). Photo with the **Big Lobster**, then a play on the jetty and beach. Off the road by 19:30.

## Day 5 · Sun 27 Dec · Kingston SE to Adelaide (~295 km, longest day)
- **09:00** Take the Princes Highway beside the **Coorong**. Stop at the **Salt Creek** roadhouse for a break and a look at the lagoon.
  - ⚠ Stay on the highway. Most Coorong side tracks are gravel or sand.
  - Kingston to Meningie is 144 km with few towns, so leave Kingston with at least half a tank.
- **About 11:00** Stop in **Meningie**: the playground on the shore of Lake Albert.
- **About 13:00** Visit **Hahndorf**: lunch, the main street and the lolly shop. Optional: strawberry picking at **Beerenberg Farm** (in season in December; check its holiday hours).
- **About 15:30** Arrive at the **Adelaide hotel**. Park, then walk to the Botanic Garden or Rundle Mall for the evening.

## Day 6 · Mon 28 Dec (public holiday) · Adelaide Hills to the airport (~45 km)
- **09:00** Check out. Drive to **Cleland Wildlife Park** (~25 min). It opens at 09:30 and only closes on Christmas Day.
  - Kangaroos and wallabies roam free and you can hand-feed them. Book a **koala close-up** session in advance.
  - Backup if Cleland closes for catastrophic fire danger: **Adelaide Zoo**.
- **12:30** Visit the **Mount Lofty Summit** lookout (5 min away). Lunch at the summit café.
- **14:30** Leave for the airport (~35 min). Fill the tank near the airport.
- **About 15:15** Return the car at Adelaide Airport. This leaves 45 minutes of buffer before the **16:00 deadline**. Your flight leaves at 18:00.

## Bookings (book this week: Christmas sells out)

   - Add a **child seat** for a 6-year-old (a forward-facing harness seat or a booster, depending on height).
   - Add the **second driver**.
   - Check the one-way fee.
2. **Apollo Bay**, 23 Dec: a family room or two queens.
3. **Port Fairy**, 24 Dec.
4. **Mount Gambier**, 25 Dec. Ask whether the restaurant serves Christmas dinner.
5. **Kingston SE**, 26 Dec. It's a small town, so book first. Fallback is Robe, but that makes 27 Dec about 4 h 10 of driving.
6. **Adelaide, IHG**, 27 Dec:
   - First choice: **Crowne Plaza Adelaide** (Frome St), two queens.

   - Ask about parking, which is usually extra.
7. **Christmas lunch**, Port Fairy, 25 Dec, 12:00.
8. **Cleland koala close-up**, 28 Dec, morning session.

All rooms: aim for A$350 a night or less, choose free cancellation where offered, and add your IHG number to the Adelaide booking.

## Checklist
- [ ] Both full licences in the car. Second driver is on the rental contract.
- [ ] Child seat fitted and checked at pickup.
- [ ] **24 Dec:** fill the tank and buy food for Christmas Day. Victorian shops are closed on 25 Dec, and SA shops have short hours on 26 Dec.
- [ ] **25 Dec:** no fresh fruit or vegetables cross into SA (fruit-fly rules, with bins at the border). Clocks go back 30 minutes.
- [ ] Respect the off-the-road times above. Kangaroos are most active at dusk.
- [ ] No gravel roads (rental rule). If the sat nav suggests a "shortcut" through the Otways or the Coorong, ignore it.
- [ ] Download offline maps. There's no phone coverage in parts of the Otways and the Coorong.
- [ ] Each morning, check the fire danger rating (VicEmergency and Alert SA apps). On extreme days, parks may close.
- [ ] Pack swimmers, hats, sunscreen and a warm layer. The coast can be windy and cool even in December.
- [ ] Expect public-holiday surcharges at cafés on 25, 26 and 28 Dec.
- [ ] **28 Dec:** refuel, return the car by 16:00, and keep the fuel receipt.

**Assumptions** (not covered in your answers): you land on a domestic flight, so the car is ready by about 09:30. The A$350 budget is per room per night. The hire company is not chosen yet. I found no IHG hotel between Melbourne and Adelaide on this route, so only the Adelaide night is IHG. Check the IHG app in case that has changed.
