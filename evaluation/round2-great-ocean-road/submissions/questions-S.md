# Before I plan: a few questions

Great trip choice. Christmas is peak season on the Great Ocean Road (school holidays start around 18 Dec, and coastal towns book out early), so with only about six weeks to go I want to get the shape right first and flag what to book quickly. Please answer what you can; "no preference" is a fine answer.

## 1. Dates and length
- What date do you leave Melbourne, and what date do you need to be in Adelaide (or back home)?
- How many days in total for the drive? (Rough guide: 4 days is the minimum to enjoy it; 5 to 7 days is comfortable.)
- Where do you want to be on Christmas Eve and Christmas Day itself? Do you want a special Christmas lunch or dinner (most restaurants need booking now and charge a holiday surcharge), or would you rather keep the day simple?

## 2. Start, finish and getting home
- Is this one-way (finishing in Adelaide), or do you need to get back to Melbourne?
- If one-way: how are you getting home from Adelaide (fly, train, someone else drives)? Have you booked it yet?
- Are you flying into Melbourne, or starting from home there?

## 3. Car and drivers
- Your own car or a rental? If rental, is it booked? (One-way Melbourne to Adelaide rentals usually carry a one-way fee, and Christmas availability is tight.)
- How many of the three of you will drive, and are all drivers comfortable on narrow, winding coastal roads? Any overseas licences?
- Any upper limit on driving per day (for example, no more than 3 or 4 hours)? Are you OK driving after dark? (I'd normally avoid it: wildlife, especially kangaroos, is a real risk at dusk on country roads.)

## 4. The three of you
- Adults only, or any children or older travellers?
- Any mobility limits that affect walks or stairs (for example the Gibson Steps or longer bush walks)?
- Any dietary needs I should plan restaurants around?

## 5. Route and what you want to see
Pick any that appeal:
- **Classic coast**: Torquay, Bells Beach, Lorne, Apollo Bay, Twelve Apostles, Loch Ard Gorge, London Bridge
- **Nature and wildlife**: Great Otway National Park, rainforest walks, waterfalls, koalas at Kennett River, Tower Hill
- **Towns and food**: Port Fairy, Warrnambool, Port Campbell, local wineries and seafood
- **Further west and inland**: Grampians National Park (a detour north, but a highlight), Mount Gambier's Blue Lake, Coonawarra wine region, Robe
- **Coorong and the last leg**: the Coorong, then into Adelaide via Murray Bridge or Victor Harbor and the Fleurieu Peninsula
- Anything else on your must-see list, or anything you'd rather skip?

## 6. Pace and style
- Relaxed (a couple of highlights a day, long lunches), balanced, or packed (see as much as possible)?
- Early starts OK? (Sunrise or early morning at the Twelve Apostles avoids the tour-bus crowds.)
- Interested in paid activities such as a helicopter flight over the Twelve Apostles, the Otway Fly Treetop Walk, surfing lessons or guided wildlife tours?

## 7. Accommodation
- What kind of places: hotels or motels, holiday rentals / cabins, caravan parks, or a mix?
- Room setup for three: one room or apartment for all three, or separate rooms (for example a twin plus a single)?
- Any hotel chain or loyalty program you'd like to use?
- Have you booked anything yet?

## 8. Budget
- Rough budget per night for accommodation (total for the three of you), and roughly for food and activities?
- Are you happy to pay more for the best-located places over Christmas, or is cost the priority?

## 9. Anything else
- Is there anything already fixed (bookings, events, people you're visiting along the way)?
- Anything you definitely want to avoid (long hikes, crowds, early mornings, swimming beaches with no patrol)?
