# Before I plan: a few questions

Happy to plan Melbourne to Adelaide via the Great Ocean Road for the three of you. To build a realistic, time-budgeted route, I need the following.

## 1. Dates and time available
- What are your exact start and end dates, and how many days can you give the drive? (For example, 24 Dec to 30 Dec.)
- Do you have a fixed time you must be in Adelaide (a flight, a Christmas lunch, a hotel check-in)?
- Roughly what time do you like to start and finish driving each day?

## 2. Start, end and return logistics
- Where exactly do you start in Melbourne (home address, airport, a hotel) and where do you finish in Adelaide?
- Is this one-way? If you are using a rental car, will you drop it in Adelaide (one-way fee), and how are you getting home or back to Melbourne afterwards (flight, train, drive back)?

## 3. Travel mode and drivers
- Are you driving your own car, a rental car, or a campervan?
- How many of the three of you can drive and share the wheel?
- What is the most driving you are comfortable with in one day (hours or km)?

## 4. Route type
Which do you prefer?
- (a) Scenic: full Great Ocean Road (Torquay to Allansford), then the coast via Port Fairy, Portland, Mount Gambier, Robe and the Coorong
- (b) Scenic first, then faster: Great Ocean Road, then cut inland on the Western/Dukes Highway
- (c) Add an inland detour (for example the Grampians) on the way
- (d) Not sure, suggest the best option for my days

## 5. Must-visit and nice-to-have stops
- Are there places you definitely want to include (for example Twelve Apostles, Loch Ard Gorge, Cape Otway Lightstation, Otway Fly treetop walk, Erskine Falls, Tower Hill, Port Fairy, Blue Lake in Mount Gambier, Umpherston Sinkhole, Naracoorte Caves, Robe, the Coorong, Hahndorf)?
- Anything you have already seen or want to skip?

## 6. Pace and dwell time
- Relaxed (few stops, long stays), balanced, or packed (see as much as possible)?
- Do you want any short walks or hikes, and how long (under 1 hour, half-day)?

## 7. Points of interest to look for along the way
Which should I find along the route? (choose any)
- Viewpoints and lookouts
- Beaches and swimming spots
- Wildlife (koalas, kangaroos, penguins, whale-watching)
- Food: cafes, bakeries, restaurants, wineries (Coonawarra, Adelaide Hills)
- Museums and cultural sites
- Fuel and rest stops only

## 8. Christmas constraints
- Do you need to be somewhere specific on Christmas Eve, Christmas Day or Boxing Day?
- Are you OK with many cafes, restaurants, attractions and some fuel stations being closed or on reduced hours on 25 December?

## 9. Accommodation
- Do you already have any overnight stays booked? If so, where and on which nights?
- If not, what type do you want (hotel, motel, cabin, Airbnb, camping) and roughly what budget per night for three people?

## 10. Anything else
- Any mobility, health, dietary or other needs I should plan around?
