# Before I plan: a few questions

I'd love to help with this. Christmas is the busiest time of year on the Great Ocean Road (school holidays start around 18 Dec in Victoria and South Australia), so rooms and one-way hire cars go fast. A few answers now will let me give you a plan you can book this week.

Short answers are fine. If you're unsure, say so and I'll use the default in brackets.

## 1. Dates
1. What day do you leave Melbourne, and what day do you need to be in Adelaide? (Christmas Day 2026 is a Friday. Boxing Day is Saturday 26 Dec, and Monday 28 Dec is the substituted public holiday.)
2. Where do you want to be on Christmas Day itself? For example, a nice Christmas lunch somewhere along the way, or already in Adelaide with family. Many shops, cafes and some attractions close on 25 Dec, and Christmas lunches need booking.
3. Are any dates fixed already, such as flights in or out, a family event, or work?

## 2. Getting there and back
4. How are you getting to Melbourne and leaving Adelaide? Flying in, flying out, or something else? If you have flights, please send the times.
5. Will it be one-way, Melbourne to Adelaide, or do you need to bring the car back to Melbourne? A one-way hire usually carries an extra fee, and returning the car means a long drive back (about 8 to 9 hours on the inland route).
6. Car: are you hiring one, or using your own? If hiring, do you have a size in mind? With three adults and their luggage I'd suggest at least a mid-size SUV or large sedan. Do you want a campervan instead?

## 3. Drivers
7. How many of the three will drive, and what licences do they hold (Australian full, Australian P-plate, or overseas)? This matters for hire age rules (under-25 fees) and for whether you need an International Driving Permit or an English translation.
8. Are the drivers comfortable on winding coastal roads? Do you want to avoid driving after dark? Kangaroos and wildlife are a real risk at dusk on rural roads. (Default: no driving after sunset, which is around 8:45 pm in late December.)

## 4. Pace and route
9. Roughly how many nights do you have for the whole trip?
10. What's the most driving you're happy with in one day? (Default: up to about 3.5 hours of driving, not counting stops.)
11. Which of these are you interested in? Pick any:
    - a. The classic coast: Torquay, Lorne, Apollo Bay, Twelve Apostles, Loch Ard Gorge
    - b. Rainforest and waterfalls in the Great Otway National Park (Erskine Falls, Otway Fly treetop walk)
    - c. Wildlife: koalas at Kennett River, the Cape Otway lightstation, Tower Hill
    - d. Port Fairy, Warrnambool, and Portland
    - e. A detour north to the Grampians for hiking and lookouts (adds about 1 to 2 days)
    - f. Mount Gambier (Blue Lake and the sinkholes) and Penola/Coonawarra wineries
    - g. The Coorong coast route into Adelaide, or a faster inland finish
    - h. Wineries elsewhere, food and markets, beaches and swimming, surfing, or photography at sunrise and sunset
12. Anything you've already done, or definitely don't want?

## 5. Where you sleep
13. What kind of accommodation do you want? Hotels or motels, holiday houses or Airbnb, caravan-park cabins, or camping. For three people: one room with three beds, a room with a sofa bed, or separate rooms?
14. Roughly what budget per night for the whole group? (For example, under A$200, A$200 to 350, or A$350+. Expect Christmas prices to be higher.)
15. Do you belong to any hotel loyalty program you'd like to use?
16. Would you rather stay put for two nights somewhere and do day trips, or move on every day?

## 6. The group
17. Who are the three of you: adults, kids (what ages), or older travellers? Are there any mobility needs, fitness limits for walks, dietary needs, or anyone who gets car sick on winding roads?
18. Any pets coming along?

## 7. Anything else
19. What's your total budget for the trip, if you have one in mind, and what's included (fuel, hire, accommodation, food, activities)?
20. Have you booked anything yet (car, accommodation, tours)? If so, please paste the details and I'll build around them.
